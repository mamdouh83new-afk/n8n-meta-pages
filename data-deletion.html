Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 9, 2026

You can ask us to delete the data N8n holds about you at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1: Remove the app from your Meta account

  1. Open your Facebook account and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and Websites (or Business Integrations).
  3. Select N8n and click Remove.

This revokes the app's access to your account and stops any further data collection.

Option 2: Email a deletion request

Send an email to mamdouh83new@gmail.com with the subject "N8n Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • Your name and the Facebook / Instagram / WhatsApp account or Page ID connected to the app.
  • A short statement that you want your data deleted.
What happens next

We will verify the request and delete the data associated with your account, including messages, identifiers and access tokens, within 30 days. We will confirm by email when it is done. Data we are legally required to keep may be retained for the legally required period only.

See also our Privacy Policy.
