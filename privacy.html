Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how N8n ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses and protects information when you interact with the app, including through Meta platforms such as Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp.

1. Information we collect

  • Account identifiers provided by Meta when you authorize the app, such as your public profile ID and name.
  • Messages and content you send to, or receive through, pages or accounts connected to the app, including message text, timestamps and sender IDs.
  • Technical data necessary to operate the service, such as access tokens, webhook event logs and error logs.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive categories of data, and we do not ask for passwords to your Meta accounts.

2. How we use information

  • To receive, process and respond to messages and events through the connected accounts.
  • To run automations and workflows that you have configured.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse and troubleshoot problems.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be processed by infrastructure and service providers that are necessary to operate the app (for example hosting and automation services), and may be disclosed if required by law. Data exchanged through Meta platforms is also subject to Meta's own policies.

4. Data retention

We keep data only as long as needed to provide the service or to meet legal obligations. Access tokens are removed when you disconnect the app, and other data can be deleted on request (see below).

5. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, such as restricted access and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time. To delete your data, follow the steps on our Data Deletion page.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision.
